Welcome

Care that feels like home

Bright Beginners gives primary school children a calm, nurturing space between the end of the school day and home time. Run with patience and love by Belinda Rodgers, every child is supervised, supported and encouraged.

We make time for homework, an afternoon meal, and plenty of free play and fun — so children arrive home happy, fed, and with their school work done.
